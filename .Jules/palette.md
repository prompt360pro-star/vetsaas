## 2026-08-28 - Dynamic ARIA bindings and Label associations in Forms\n**Learning:** When building standard form elements, native labels, and dynamically generated IDs (via `useId`) mapped to `htmlFor`, `id`, and `aria-describedby` are crucial for robust accessibility. Decorative icons and asterisks must have `aria-hidden='true'` to avoid cluttering screen reader output.\n**Action:** Always generate stable fallback IDs with `useId()` in UI components to ensure forms are inherently accessible without relying on developers to explicitly pass them.
