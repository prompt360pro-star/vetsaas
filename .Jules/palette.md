## 2024-03-30 - Accordion Accessibility Improvements\n**Learning:** The existing Accordion component lacked critical ARIA attributes (e.g., `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `role="region"`) and visible focus states, making it difficult for keyboard and screen reader users to navigate.\n**Action:** Ensure custom disclosure widgets like `Accordion` include `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` on the trigger, `role="region"` and `aria-labelledby` on the content wrapper, and appropriate `focus-visible` utility classes for proper keyboard navigation and accessibility.
